Why Terana exists

Terana joins Terre (earth) and Ana (back): restoring the earth to balance.

Feral animals and pests cause economic, environmental and social harm. We reduce that harm so native flora and fauna, and the people who share the land with them, are better off.

Terana works to restore balance: protecting native flora and fauna and the environment they depend on.

Terana Environmental is directed by Courtney Kelly, a licensed vertebrate pest management technician (WA Department of Health). We work across Western Australia, with programs tailored for growers, commercial sites, councils and larger landholders.

Services

Each service has its own page with the detail.

Surveys, data and planning

  • Fox den detection

    Fox den location by a handler and trained detection dog, with every site recorded and mapped.

  • Pest management plans and IPM programs

    Evidence-based management plans and integrated programs, written and delivered.

  • Field necropsy

    Sex, weight, reproductive status and stomach contents recorded from animals removed, to inform the program. Any other details can be collected on request.

  • GIS and mapping

    Survey tracks, recorded sites and control work mapped and supplied as a mapping file.

  • Monitoring and camera surveillance

    Camera surveillance, thermal surveys and drone surveys, before and after control.

Feral animal control

  • Vertebrate pest management

    Overview of the species we manage and the methods we use.

  • Fox control

    Den detection, den fumigation, 1080 baiting, canid pest ejectors, trapping and shooting.

  • Rabbit control

    1080 and pindone baiting, calicivirus release, warren fumigation, warren ripping, Rodenator and shooting.

  • Feral cat control

    Trapping and shooting, with monitoring before and after.

  • Feral deer control

    Ground shooting with thermal optics for night work.

  • Feral pig control

    1080 and sodium nitrite baiting, trapping and shooting.

  • Bird management

    Pigeon and pest bird programs: assessment, control, decontamination, deterrents and reporting.

Wildlife and habitat

  • Native fauna impact management

    Kangaroo, emu and corella impacts: non-lethal options first, authorised control where needed.

  • Habitat creation

    Nest boxes and carved tree hollows, custom built for the target species and the site.

  • Snake removal and relocation

    24/7 call-outs within 60 minutes of Perth CBD, and snake management for larger sites across WA.

  • Bee removal and relocation

    Swarms and hives removed safely and relocated to a beekeeper.

We manage feral animals generally. If the species on your site is not listed, ask us.
